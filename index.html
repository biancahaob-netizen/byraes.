Where are you starting?

Pick the one that sounds most like you. Different starting points need different next steps.

If you're starting from scratch

Start with the why. The how gets easier after.

You don't need to know every platform feature or trend to begin. What helps most is understanding the basics: what social media marketing is for, who a post is talking to, and why some content is useful while some just fills the feed.

Normal lang malito sa umpisa. Most beginner confusion comes from hearing ten tips at once without knowing which one matters first.

Your next step

Skim the "Social media fundamentals" topic below, then check out the 14-day Starter. It's being designed for exactly this stage: one idea and one small task per day.

See the 14-day Starter plan

Good to know: no paid tools needed to learn the basics. A phone, a notes app, and a business you can practice on is enough.

If you're exploring it as a career

Learn the work, not just the job title.

Social media management is more than posting. It usually involves planning, writing, coordinating approvals, replying to comments, and checking what's working. Knowing what those deliverables look like will help you decide if this path fits you.

The best way to find out? Practice on a real or made-up brand, like a friend's bakery or a café you invent, and actually build the plan.

Your next step

Look at "Content planning and creation" and "Workflows, tools, and templates" below. The 14-day Starter is also planned to end with a beginner social media plan for a practice brand.
